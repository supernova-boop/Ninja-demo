Cut by craft, not by clock

Fade & Co. opened in 2014 above a tailor's shop on Marlow Street with two chairs and one idea: a haircut should take as long as it takes.

Our barbers train for two years before they take a walk-in. We cut every guard from #0 to #8, keep the razors honed on leather, and know most of our regulars by name and by head shape.

Grab a coffee, pick a record, and take the chair.
